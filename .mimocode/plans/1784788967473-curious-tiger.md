# Plan: Push ellipse up on mobile — reduce top spacing

## Problem
Too much empty space between the navbar and the ellipse on mobile. `padding-top: calc(80px + env(safe-area-inset-top))` and `margin-top: 20px` on `.ch-hero-inner` are pushing content down.

## Fix in `app/globals.css` (mobile breakpoint)

| Selector | Property | Current | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.ch-hero` | `padding-top` | `calc(80px + env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px))` | `calc(40px + env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px))` |
| `.ch-hero-inner` | `margin-top` | `20px` | `0` |

## Verification
- Mobile view: ellipse starts closer to navbar, less dead space above
- "Trusted by" still visible at bottom
